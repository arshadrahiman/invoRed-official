Engineering Protocol

The terms, in writing, before anything starts.

The same transparent framework for a web system, a custom GenAI model, mobile engineering, or a design system.

  1. 01

    A fixed price, before we begin.

    Quoted per project sprint in writing. There are no surprise hourly invoices or opaque overages, because every milestone is scoped precisely.

  2. 02

    Half to start, the rest on production deployment.

    Engineering begins once the initial sprint deposit arrives. Nothing outside the agreed specification is billed without your explicit prior sign-off.

  3. 03

    Two comprehensive refinement rounds included.

    User feedback, QA testing, and design adjustments are integrated into every milestone deliverable before final release.

  4. 04

    100% intellectual property & code ownership.

    Every repository commit, architectural diagram, and design token belongs to you. Zero proprietary lock-in.
